[REGULATORY CONTEXT — Malaysia]
CSA 2024 / NACSA NCII:
- Section 29 CSA 2024: NCII entities must report to NACSA within 24 hours.
- Full report required within 14 days with complete investigation findings.
- Report portal: https://myresponse.nacsa.gov.my

PDPA 2010 / PDPC:
- Section 14A (amendment): notify PDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach.
- Notification to affected data subjects required where likely to cause harm.
- Contact: pdp@kkmm.gov.my

BNM RMiT (licensed financial institutions):
- Technology Risk Management (RMiT) para 10.55: notify BNM immediately upon discovering
  a material cyber incident. "Material" includes: disruption to banking operations,
  fraud, data breach affecting customer data, or system compromise.
