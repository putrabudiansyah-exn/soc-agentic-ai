CSA 2024 / NACSA:
- Section 29: NCII entities must comply with cybersecurity measures prescribed by NACSA.
- NCII sectors required to undergo annual cybersecurity risk assessment.
- Minimum security baseline: asset inventory, vulnerability management, access control, logging.

PDPA 2010:
- Section 5: Personal data must be protected with security safeguards appropriate to the harm.
- Section 11: Data users must maintain records of personal data processed.
- Section 14A: Report breaches to PDPC within 72 hours.

BNM RMiT:
- Para 10.08: Technology assets supporting material business functions must be inventoried.
- Para 10.32: Vulnerability assessments on internet-facing systems at least annually.
