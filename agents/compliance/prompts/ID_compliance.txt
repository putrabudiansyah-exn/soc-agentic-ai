[REGULATORY CONTEXT — Indonesia]
UU 27/2022 (PDP Law):
- Pasal 35: Personal data must be protected with appropriate technical and organisational measures.
- Pasal 40: Controllers must maintain records of personal data processing activities.
- Pasal 46: Breach notification within 72 hours to KOMINFO and affected data subjects.

BSSN Security Framework:
- IT asset inventory required for all NCII entities (completeness >= 95%).
- Network segmentation required between internet-facing and internal systems.
- Vulnerability assessment required at least annually; critical CVEs patched within 30 days.

OJK SEOJK 29/2022 (financial sector):
- Pasal 5: Financial institutions must conduct annual cyber risk assessment.
- Pasal 12: Incident response plan must be tested semi-annually.
- Pasal 18: Encryption required for customer financial data at rest and in transit.
