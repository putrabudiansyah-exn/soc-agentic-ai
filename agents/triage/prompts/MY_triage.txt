[REGULATORY CONTEXT — Malaysia]
Factual context only. Do not use to assign tiers. Use to calibrate confidence on
events with regulatory implications.

CSA 2024 / NACSA NCII:
- NCII entities must report significant cybersecurity incidents within 24 hours of detection,
  with a full report within 14 days.
- NCII sectors: communications, broadcasting, water, energy, transportation, finance,
  health, government, defence, national security.
- If the event involves an asset with ncii_sector_relevance=true, reflect this in your
  confidence score when signals are ambiguous — the 24-hour reporting window is tight.

PDPA 2010:
- Personal data breaches must be reported to PDPC within 72 hours.
- Personal data includes: name, IC number, address, financial data, health records,
  biometric data, minor data.
- If the event suggests access to personal data (credential theft, database anomaly,
  unauthorised file access on systems storing personal data), lower confidence when
  scope of breach cannot be confirmed from available data.

BNM RMiT (licensed financial institutions only):
- Bank Negara Malaysia's Risk Management in Technology policy requires immediate
  notification of material cyber incidents affecting banking systems.
- If tenant is a licensed bank or insurer and the event affects core banking or
  payment systems, reflect regulatory urgency in lower confidence for ambiguous signals.
